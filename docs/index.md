# Facts Robot

Facts Robot is a **personal, non-commercial tool** used by a single creator to publish short educational
"did you know" fact videos to their **own** YouTube channel and Instagram account.

What it does:

- Each morning it finds an interesting, trending fact and checks it against trusted sources
  (for example Wikipedia, NASA and science news sites).
- It sends the creator video prompts, a short script, music suggestions and a caption on Telegram.
- After the creator makes the video and sends it back, it uploads that video to the creator's own
  YouTube channel and Instagram account, and later reads basic statistics (views, likes, shares)
  for those videos.

It is not offered to the public and has no other users.
